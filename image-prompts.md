# Image Prompts for Pravah Landing Page

These are the detailed prompts for the placeholder images used in the landing page. Generate these images and place them in the `public` directory with the specified filenames.

## 1. Hero Image / Dashboard Mockup
**Filename**: `hero-dashboard.png`
**Prompt**: A clean, professional, high-fidelity UI mockup of an enterprise government dashboard for a "Smart Grievance Resolution" system. The design should feature a bright, crisp white background with official navy blue and subtle green accents. It shows a clear data table of citizen complaints, an "SLA Status" indicator, and a structured workflow pipeline (Understand -> Plan -> Act). Clean sans-serif typography (like Inter or Roboto), highly legible, extremely professional government tech aesthetic. Absolutely no dark mode, no glowing elements, no glassmorphism, and no "AI/cyberpunk" aesthetics. No text other than realistic UI labels.

## 2. Pothole "Before" Evidence Image
**Filename**: `evidence-before.png`
**Prompt**: A realistic, daylight photo of a large pothole on an urban asphalt road. The photo should look like it was taken by a citizen on a smartphone during the day. The pothole is deep and poses a clear hazard. High resolution, clear, natural lighting, professional civic documentation style.

## 3. Pothole "After" Evidence Image
**Filename**: `evidence-after.png`
**Prompt**: A realistic, daylight photo of the exact same road location as the previous image, but the pothole has been freshly repaired with new dark asphalt. The patch is smooth and level with the rest of the road. Natural lighting, high resolution, clear details showing a successful municipal repair job.

## 4. Multi-Agent System Diagram 
**Filename**: `agent-workflow.png`
**Prompt**: A clean, professional flowchart diagram on a white background illustrating a multi-agent system. The diagram should use standard, professional enterprise symbols and arrows connecting clearly labeled functional blocks (Triage Agent, Resolution Planner, Evidence Agent). Use a strict color palette of white, light gray, and official navy blue. Minimalist, corporate, academic diagram style. No glowing nodes, no dark backgrounds, no neon colors.
